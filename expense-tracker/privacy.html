Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

Information stored by the website

Expense Tracker stores the transactions you enter in your browser's local storage. This information is used to display your balance, transaction list, and spending breakdown.

Information sent to a server

This static version of Expense Tracker does not send your transaction data to a server or require an account.

Cookies and local storage

The website uses browser local storage to keep your transactions available when you return to the site on the same browser and device.

Your control

You can delete individual transactions or clear all transactions from the tracker. You can also clear the site's local storage through your browser settings.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the website's data practices change. The latest version will be posted on this page.
